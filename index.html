About

Engineer & Builder

I am Abhinav Abraham, an engineer with a passion for building systems that sit at the intersection of autonomous systems, machine learning, and physical hardware. From designing ruggedized sensor packages with custom PCBs and machined enclosures to training perception models and shipping navigation stacks in ROS 2, I enjoy tackling problems where hardware and software have to work together under real constraints.

My Ph.D. research at the University of Illinois Chicago spans closed-loop control, real-time ML inference pipelines, embedded electronics, and multi-modal sensing - carried out in a DoD-funded program alongside UW-Madison and the U.S. Army Research Laboratory. I have field-deployed a sensor I built inline with a running engine test cell, designed the PCB that controls it, and written the ML pipeline that processes its output in under six seconds.

During my M.S., I built a fully autonomous robotic control system - fluorescence microscopy feeding an ML classifier commanding a robotic actuator, with no human in the loop. That architecture has stayed with me as a design principle: sense, decide, act, repeat.

Outside formal research, I build agentic AI pipelines, ROS 2 navigation stacks, EKF state estimators from real UAV flight data, and computer vision benchmarks - all on GitHub.

Research & Engineering

Lynch Lab, UIC
Research Engineer - Robotics, ML & Sensing
DoD VICTOR Program UW-Madison U.S. Army CCDC-ARL
  • Co-designed a compact ruggedized sensor system (70 x 131 x 84 mm, 890 g) alongside one other engineer - machined aluminum flow cell with optical windows, sealing faces, and fluidic ports; full sensor package assembly integrating a spectrometer, pump, solenoid valves, CCD array, single-board processor, and signal conditioning electronics. MIL-STD-810 and MIL-STD-461 compliant. Pressure tested at 103.4 kPag. Deployed inline with a running engine test cell at UW-Madison.
  • Designed custom PCB (KiCad) integrated with NI DAQ for test bench automation: solenoid valve sequencing, diagnostic trigger synchronization, and real-time data acquisition - eliminated manual triggering errors from high-pressure test cycles.
  • Built end-to-end real-time ML inference pipeline: automated spectral preprocessing, chemometric feature extraction, multi-model regression (MLR, SVR, random forest, gradient boosting, CNN, ensemble methods with bootstrap aggregation and stacking); real-time output under 6 seconds, under 10% error on out-of-distribution real-world samples. FastAPI backend with operator GUI. Patent pending 2026.
  • Built gravimetric mixture preparation apparatus (±0.05 g accuracy) for formulating 79 surrogate blends from 23 pure components; designed a custom modular spectrometer with interchangeable diffraction grating, Astrolab CCD array, machined mounts, and alignment stages.
  • Developed ML framework predicting full spectral signatures directly from SMILES strings and composition fractions (ElasticNet + PCA, 99.9% variance retained); test R² 0.979 mean / 0.996 median on 120 held-out samples, challenge set R² 0.995 on 41 unseen mixtures. Manuscript in preparation.
  • Built physics-constrained spectral modeling pipeline combining DFT-computed reference spectra (Gaussian16 on HPC) with Indirect Hard Modeling; automated anomaly flagging; sub-5% reconstruction error. Published: Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory Systems (2025).
  • Developed automated emission spectroscopy pipeline with ab initio calibration and quality gating across thousands of spectra; ±100 K accuracy. Peters Fellowship 2022. Published: Proceedings of the Combustion Institute (2023).
