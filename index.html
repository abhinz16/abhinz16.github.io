About

Engineer & Builder

I am Abhinav Abraham, an engineer with a passion for building systems that sit at the intersection of autonomous systems, machine learning, and physical hardware. From designing ruggedized sensor packages with custom PCBs and machined enclosures to training perception models and shipping navigation stacks in ROS 2, I enjoy tackling problems where hardware and software have to work together under real constraints.

My Ph.D. research at the University of Illinois Chicago spans closed-loop control, real-time ML inference pipelines, embedded electronics, and multi-modal sensing - carried out in a DoD-funded program alongside UW-Madison and the U.S. Army Research Laboratory. I have field-deployed a sensor I built inline with a running engine test cell, designed the PCB that controls it, and written the ML pipeline that processes its output in under six seconds.

During my M.S., I built a fully autonomous robotic control system - fluorescence microscopy feeding an ML classifier commanding a robotic actuator, with no human in the loop. That architecture has stayed with me as a design principle: sense, decide, act, repeat.

Outside formal research, I build agentic AI pipelines, ROS 2 navigation stacks, EKF state estimators from real UAV flight data, and computer vision benchmarks - all on GitHub.
